## 4.1 Cooling system description
The cooling system uses a pressurized coolant circuit with a radiator, an expansion tank, a thermostat and a belt-driven fan. Normal operating coolant temperature is between 80 and 95 degrees Celsius.

## 4.2 Coolant specification
Use only ethylene glycol based coolant with a 50/50 mixture of coolant and demineralized water. Never mix coolant brands.

## 4.3 Troubleshooting high coolant temperature
When the high temperature alarm is active, check the coolant level first, then inspect the radiator core and fins for blockage by dust or mud, and check the fan belt tension and wear. If these are correct, test the thermostat opening temperature. Never open the radiator cap while the engine is hot: the system is under pressure.

## 5.1 Hydraulic system description
The hydraulic system supplies the steering, the brakes and the dump body hoist. It uses a main hydraulic tank, a pump driven by the engine and return filters. Check the hydraulic tank level daily with the dump body lowered.
